# Autosweep companion app — UX case study

Heuristic UX audit and proposed redesign of the Autosweep RFID mobile app
(San Miguel Infrastructure tollways, 1M+ registered customers).

## Contents

| Path | What it is |
|---|---|
| `autosweep-ux-audit.html` | Full audit: 32 prioritised findings, journeys, screen-by-screen review, design solutions, missing features, quick wins, research questions. Open in a browser. |
| `screens/original/` | 31 captures of the current app (IMG_8041–8071): splash, home, vehicle info, SOA, account/reload, traffic, menu, announcement, password, customer care, legal. |
| `screens/reload-flow/` | 10 captures of the end-to-end reload flow (R01–R10): credit card, add card, direct debit, payment summary, bank login, e-wallet, GCash handoff. |
